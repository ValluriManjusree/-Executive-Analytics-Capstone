### Slide 1 – Introduction

“Hello everyone. My name is Sai Aishwarya.

This presentation is about my Executive Analytics Capstone on **Customer Churn Analytics**.

The main objective of this project is to analyze customer behavior, identify customers who may be at higher risk of churn, and recommend actions that can help the business improve customer retention.”

### Slide 2 – Business Problem

“The business problem is customer churn.

When customers leave, the company can lose recurring revenue and may also need to spend more money acquiring new customers.

So, the goal of this analysis is to understand the factors associated with churn and create a simple, explainable way to prioritize customers for retention actions.”

### Slide 3 – Executive Snapshot

“This analysis uses a sample of 15 customers.

I examined customer tenure, monthly charges, total charges, contract type, and support tickets.

The analysis focuses on identifying patterns in churn and converting those patterns into actionable customer-risk priorities.”

### Slide 4 – Insight 1: Contract Type

“The first important insight is related to contract type.

Customers on different contract types show different churn behavior in this sample.

Month-to-month customers are particularly important to monitor because their contracts provide more flexibility to leave.

Therefore, the business can consider targeted retention offers or contract-upgrade campaigns for customers showing additional risk signals.”

### Slide 5 – Insight 2: Tenure and Support Activity

“The second insight is related to customer tenure and support activity.

Newer customers and customers with higher support activity can require more attention.

A high number of support tickets may indicate unresolved issues or customer dissatisfaction.

Therefore, proactive onboarding and faster issue resolution can be used as retention actions.”

### Slide 6 – Insight 3: Customer Segmentation

“I created four customer segments using tenure and support activity.

These segments are **Loyal and Stable, New and High Support, Newer Customers, and Established Customers**.

The purpose of segmentation is to avoid treating every customer in the same way.

Different customer groups can receive different retention strategies based on their characteristics.”

### Slide 7 – Insight 4: Explainable Churn-Risk Score

“I also created an explainable churn-risk score.

The score uses three signals.

Short tenure adds 2 points, month-to-month contract adds 2 points, and higher-than-median support tickets add 1 point.

Based on the total score, customers are classified as low, medium, or high risk.

This is a prioritization model rather than a guaranteed prediction of churn.”

### Slide 8 – Recommended Actions and Impact

“Based on the analysis, I recommend prioritizing high-risk customers for retention activities.

For newer customers, the business can provide proactive onboarding and support.

For month-to-month customers, targeted engagement or contract-upgrade campaigns can be considered.

For customers with frequent support tickets, resolving their issues quickly should be a priority.

The estimated impact should be treated as an illustrative scenario because the dataset is very small and does not provide enough evidence for a reliable financial forecast.”

### Slide 9 – Limitations and Next Steps

“There are some important limitations.

The dataset contains only 15 customers, so the findings cannot be generalized to the entire customer population.

The risk score is rule-based and is designed for prioritization, not as a production prediction model.

For future work, I would use a larger historical dataset, validate the model on unseen customers, and measure whether retention actions actually reduce churn.

Thank you.”
